# **References for Comparative Methods**

This document provides links to the official papers and implementations for all comparative methods used in this study.  

## **1. U-Net**
- Paper: [Convolutional Networks for Biomedical Image Segmentation](https://link.springer.com/chapter/10.1007/978-3-319-24574-4_28)
- Official Implementation: [https://lmb.informatik.uni-freiburg.de/people/ronneber/u-net/](https://lmb.informatik.uni-freiburg.de/people/ronneber/u-net/)
- PyTorch Implementation: [https://github.com/milesial/Pytorch-UNet](https://github.com/milesial/Pytorch-UNet)

## **2. Attention U-Net**
- Paper: [Attention U-Net: Learning Where to Look for the Pancreas](https://openreview.net/forum?id=Skft7cijM)
- Official Implementation: [https://github.com/ozan-oktay/Attention-Gated-Networks](https://github.com/ozan-oktay/Attention-Gated-Networks)

## **3. TransUNet**
- Paper: [TransUNet: Transformers Make Strong Encoders for Medical Image Segmentation](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S1361841524002056)
- Official Implementation: [https://github.com/Beckschen/TransUNet](https://github.com/Beckschen/TransUNet)

## **4. Swin-Unet**
- Paper: [Swin-Unet: Unet-like Pure Transformer for Medical Image Segmentation](https://link.springer.com/chapter/10.1007/978-3-031-25066-8_9)
- Official Implementation: [https://github.com/HuCaoFighting/Swin-Unet](https://github.com/HuCaoFighting/Swin-Unet)

## **5. U-Mamba**
- Paper: [U-Mamba: Enhancing Long-range Dependency for Biomedical Image Segmentation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.04722)
- Official Implementation: [https://github.com/bowang-lab/U-Mamba](https://github.com/bowang-lab/U-Mamba)

## **6. VM-UNet**
- Paper: [VM-UNet: Vision Mamba UNet for Medical Image Segmentation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.02491)
- Official Implementation: [https://github.com/JCruan519/VM-UNet](https://github.com/JCruan519/VM-UNet)

## **7. HMT-UNet**
- Paper: [HMT-UNet: A hybird Mamba-Transformer Vision UNet for Medical Image Segmentation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.11289)
- Official Implementation: [https://github.com/simzhangbest/HMT-Unet](https://github.com/simzhangbest/HMT-Unet)
